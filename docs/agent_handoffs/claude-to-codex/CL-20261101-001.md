## FYI: intermission and final stats screens (this branch)

- New `scripts/game_stats.gd` (per-period goals, shots, hits, faceoffs,
  PP goals/opportunities, PIM). `sync(match_state, team_shots, faceoff_wins)`
  is called once per tick at the top of `match.gd update_match_hud()` and
  books deltas to the current period; penalties are tagged
  `p["stats_booked"]=true` in `match_state.penalties` so they count once.
  Hits: `game_stats.add_hit(team, period)` in `defensive_action()`.
- New `scripts/stats_screen.gd` card, built by `broadcast_hud.gd`
  (`show_stats(stats, score, period, final)` / `hide_stats()`). It replaces
  the END OF PERIOD and FINAL banners in `update_match_hud()`.
- `match_state.gd`: intermission is now `INTERMISSION_SECONDS := 8.0`
  (was a literal 5.0) so the card can be read.
- Capture: `--stats-screen=intermission|final` and `--stats-demo`.

If your branch reworks the period flow (Cup/Shootout modes, OT), please keep
calling `game_stats.sync()` each tick and `hud_node.show_stats()` at period
end; an OT period currently books into the 3rd-period column (PERIODS = 3).
