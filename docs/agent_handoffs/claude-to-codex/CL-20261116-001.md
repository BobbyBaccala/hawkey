The owner asked for realistic CPU movement: where players play positionally
and how the game flows. AI/simulation is normally yours, so here is what
changed on `prototype/skating-puck-lab`, measured with a new CPU-vs-CPU
recorder (`tools/ai_flow_probe.gd` + `tools/ai_flow_report.py`).

## Findings (before)
- The puck was loose 79% of open play; carries lasted ~0.3 s: CPUs re-decided
  every reaction tick and passed/dumped almost at once.
- A global 0.42 s pickup cooldown after every release let short passes slide
  through the receiver; passes were led for 17 m/s though sent at 10-17 m/s.
  ~55% of passes reached a team-mate.
- Pucks that died behind a net could freeze play for 100 s: chasers pressed
  into the back of the goal frame.
- Wingers/D used fixed rink sides, so one team's LW played on his right (and
  lined up opposite the other LW at faceoffs).
- Offensive zone: D almost never shot from the point, pressured forwards
  turned away and shielded, passes kept circulating; only the chaser
  forechecked, so a team holding the puck in its own zone was never pressured.

## Changes
- `skater_ai.gd`: carry commitment (CARRY_COMMIT*), pass lane safety by
  pressure (PASS_SAFE_*), `pass_speed()` + speed-aware `lead_point()`,
  `around_nets()`, offensive-zone shot floor / point shot / reset-to-point
  pass and no "advance" term for passes in the zone.
- `team_tactics.gd`: `side_for(slot, attack)` (own left/right), wide strong-
  side point + weak-side D sliding to the middle, net front always filled
  (`carrier_slot` argument), own-zone wingers on the half-wall lane/sagging,
  1-2-2 forecheck (strong-side F on the wall, the other high).
- `match.gd` (small, described in the commits): carry_time/is_d in the AI
  context, release grace (passer 0.42 s, team-mates 0.08 s, opponents 0.25 s),
  `around_nets` on every CPU target, carrier slot to `support_target`, gap
  side and faceoff spots mirrored by attack direction, first-time shot on a
  loose puck gained in a prime spot.

## Result (4-min CPU games, 2 seeds)
Shot attempts 2.5/min and even between the teams (was 8:1), passes ~8/min,
possessions ~3.3 s, 7 possession changes/min, zone time 36/30/34 in one game.
All suites pass. If you build on the AI on your branch, these are worth
taking; tell me if any of it conflicts with your carrying/receiving work.
