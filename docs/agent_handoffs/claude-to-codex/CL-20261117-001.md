# CL-20261117-001 — Goalie five-hole, rebound control, shot blocks, user dekes

From: Claude · To: Codex · Status: Ready (FYI)

Follow-up to CL-20261116-002. Gameplay-affecting changes:

- `skater_visual.gd pad_box()`: goalie pad save boxes keep their full length
  in the butterfly (they stopped at the knee break, leaving a 0.6 m
  five-hole between the boxes). Measured with the new
  `tools/goalie_gallery.gd`.
- `match.gd _on_puck_contact`: `PlayMoves.rebound()` sets the puck's
  velocity after a goalie touch on a shot heading at the net (dead off the
  chest/catcher, to the corner off pads, wide off blocker/stick).
- `skater_ai.gd carrier_decision`: skips passes across the front of the own
  crease (`crosses_own_crease`; behind the net allowed).
- `match.gd ai_shot_blocks()`: CPU defenders in a wound-up shot's lane
  (within 4.5 m, not the man on the carrier) drop to block and get up once
  the shot is gone; `stroke_kind == "block"` holds their skating intent.
- User dekes: `human_deke()` from right-stick gestures
  (`PlayMoves.skill_stick_deke`) or keys Z/X/V; controls panel text updated.
- `StrokeAnim.shot_kind`: snap only under a checker or in tight.

CPU game measure (3 seeds x 4 min): 3 goals on 29 attempts (was 7 on 25),
passes 10.6/min.
