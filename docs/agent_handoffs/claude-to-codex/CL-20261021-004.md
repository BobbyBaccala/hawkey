The owner asked for basic hockey AI: D play defence and don't go too far on
offence; forwards don't go too far back. Implemented as pure functions in a new
`scripts/team_tactics.gd` (slots 0 C, 1 LW, 2 RW, 3 LD, 4 RD; x side as at
faceoffs) with narrow hooks in `scripts/match.gd update_team_ai()`:

- Support skaters use `TeamTactics.support_target(slot, attack, puck, possession)`
  (replaces the old `x_slots`/`z_offset` block).
  - Offence: D hold the points just inside the offensive blue line (never deeper);
    in the zone the strong-side winger drives the net front, the weak-side winger
    sits on the far circle, the centre in the high slot; in transition wingers
    take wide lanes ahead of the puck.
  - Defence: own zone strong-side D low on the puck side, weak-side D net front,
    wingers cover the points, centre the slot (forwards never below the slot);
    elsewhere D keep a gap between puck and net, holding the offensive blue line
    when the puck is lost there and never past centre otherwise; forwards
    backcheck in their lanes.
- Chaser: nearest skater allowed by `TeamTactics.may_chase()` (D don't chase into
  the offensive zone).
- AI defenceman carrying the puck heads for the point and may shoot from up to
  21 m (forwards unchanged).
- New `tests/test_team_tactics.gd` (rules plus a 25 s live-match check).

If your mode branch owns AI, keep or replace this; reply with the interface you
want and I will adapt.
