# CL-20261119-001 — Neutral-zone faceoff D stagger; goalie read time

From: Claude · To: Codex · Status: Ready (FYI)

## Neutral-zone faceoffs (`match.gd setup_faceoff`, non-end-zone dots)
Before this change, both D stood 8 m straight back from the dot, level with
each other, and the team whose blue line was behind the dot sat 6 m deep in
its own zone. Now:
- the strong-side D stands just behind the outside winger
  (`FACEOFF_NZ_D_STRONG`);
- the other D is the safety, deeper in the middle of the ice
  (`FACEOFF_NZ_D_SAFETY`).

Centre ice and the end-zone alignment (CL-20261118-001) are unchanged. There
is a new capture option, `--faceoff-neutral`.

`tests/test_faceoff_alignment.gd` now covers centre ice and all four
neutral-zone dots.

## Goalie read time (`match.gd update_goalkeepers`)
The goalie used to choose and start the exact save on the frame of release,
so only one-timers ever beat him clean. The sim goal logs (2026-11-19,
seeds 11-14) showed 1.75 goals/team at a .934 save %.

Now a shot has to be in flight for `GOALIE_READ` (0.16 s) before he commits
to a save. Until then he holds what he was doing. A puck crossing within
`GOALIE_READ_BODY` (0.30 m) of his centre is read at once: his stance and
stick already cover it, and without this rule five-hole shots from 8 m went
in.

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `goalie_gallery.gd` save % (reps 3) | 100 % | 94 % (goals are corner shots from the circle or one-timers) |
| Goals/team, 4 full games | 1.75 | 2.38 |
| Save % | .934 | .908 |
| SOG/team | 26.4 | 25.9 |
| Players at ≥ 1 P/GP | 0 | 3 |

A 4-game batch still swings about ±0.5 goals a team between batches.
