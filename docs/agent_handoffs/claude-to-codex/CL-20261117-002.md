# CL-20261117-002 — NHL-like game stats: goalie save physics, ratings, assists

From: Claude · To: Codex · Status: Ready (FYI)

Owner request: 5-minute periods (the existing 20:00 clock at clock_rate 4)
should produce NHL box scores - 25-30 shots on goal a team, goalies between
.870 and .930, the odd point-per-game player. New measuring tools:
`tools/sim_game.gd` (full CPU game, box score, per-goal log) and
`tools/sim_report.py`; `tools/goalie_gallery.gd` gained high-corner cases.

Gameplay-affecting changes:
- Goalie save bodies: upper-arm and forearm boxes added to
  `get_goalie_gear()` (zone "arm"; lab.gd builds bodies from that dict).
- `match.gd _on_puck_contact`: ~75% of chest and ~70% of catcher saves are
  frozen for a whistle (`PlayMoves.freezes`); goalie covers slow loose pucks
  within reach (`PlayMoves.goalie_covers`, wider in the butterfly).
- Every goalie touch is rebound-controlled (also the second touch inside
  `goalie_contact_timer`, which used to fall through to raw physics and
  bounce in off the back of his gear); a puck touched at the line is
  squeezed for a whistle (`goalie_squeeze`, `PlayMoves.squeezes`).
- Shots on goal count only real shots or tipped shots (not dump-ins or
  passes into the goalie).
- `SkaterAI.ONE_TIMER_BAR` 0.16 (was 0.08 inline) and `REBOUND_BAR` 0.22
  for loose-puck first-time shots.
- Rosters gain `shooting` (by line/pair) and `assists`;
  `player_skill()` folds shooting into shot_bar/shot_noise, and shot power.
  Goals and assists credited from `touch_chain` (`credit_goal`); scoring
  line on the stats card (`stats_screen.scorers`).
- Auto line changes pick the freshest unit with top units favoured
  (`next_unit`), and defence pairs now rotate too (they never did).

Note: physics runs at 120 Hz; probes counting frames at 60 Hz halve rates.
