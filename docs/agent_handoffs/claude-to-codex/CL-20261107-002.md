## Owner decision (please read before more mode work)

The owner asked today to **limit the game to a Play Now mode**, which will one
day be online with every player controlled by a person, CPUs filling any spot
until a human takes it, and **very good, realistic CPU play**. Your GM, Cup,
Shootout and Practice work on `codex/game-modes` / `codex/modes-session-20261102`

game's menu. If you keep working on gameplay, the useful targets are now
online seats/lobby and AI quality inside Play Now.

## What exists on this branch

- `scripts/control_slots.gd` — seat model. 2 teams × 6 positions (C, LW, RW,
  LD, RD, G). `claim(human, team, position, style)`, `join(human, team)`,
  `release(human)`, `move(human, position)` (team-style switching),
  `to_dict()/from_dict()` (lobby snapshot). Owner ids: `"local:1"` for the
  local player; a network lobby should use `"net:<peer>"`. Styles: `team`
  (switch to the best-placed skater) and `position` (locked, online-style).
- `scripts/skater_ai.gd` — pure CPU decision functions (shot quality, pass
  lanes, carrier choice, pursuit, gap, coverage, interceptions, four skill
  levels). `match.gd update_team_ai()` drives every skater not in a human
  seat with them.
- `match.gd`: `setup_seats()`, `apply_play_now(options)`, `human_controls(actor)`,
  `seat_actor()`, `user_team()` now follows the seat (the human can play
  either club). Command line: `--spectate`, `--team=`, `--style=`,
  `--position=`, `--difficulty=`.
- Title screen: PLAY NOW plus team / control / position / CPU skill;
  Practice was removed from the menu (practice.tscn still exists).
- Tests: `tests/test_play_now_ai.gd` (seats, AI functions, a 90 s CPU-vs-CPU
  stretch, human seat on the away team).
