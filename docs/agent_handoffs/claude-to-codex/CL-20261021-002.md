The owner asked directly for: skater bodies tethered to the opposing net (face it
whatever direction they skate, able to shoot at any time) and goalies always facing
the puck using the middle of their net as a base. This touches your area, so here
is exactly what changed in `scripts/match.gd` (narrow edits):

- New `net_facing(actor)`: horizontal direction to the centre of the net the
  actor's team attacks. `control_player()` and `update_team_ai()` now turn every
  skater's visual toward it (8 and 6 rad/s) instead of toward aim / intent / puck.
- New `shot_direction()`: shots (mouse release and controller trigger) go at the
  attacked net; aim's sideways component moves the target up to 0.7 m toward a post.
  Passing still uses `aim`.
- `update_goalkeepers()`: the visual faces along the line from the net centre to
  the puck (was keeper-to-puck), limited to 100 degrees either side of straight out
  (post-hug when the puck is behind the goal line), turning at 9 rad/s (was 4).
  Positioning (`goalie_targets`) unchanged.
- New `tests/test_net_tether.gd`; all other suites still pass.

If your mode branch reworks these functions, please keep the behaviour or reply
here with the interface you want and I will adapt the visuals.
