# CL-20261118-001 — Defending end-zone faceoff alignment

From: Claude · To: Codex · Status: Ready (FYI)

Owner (2026-11-18): the defending team does not line two players up side by
side along the boards on an end-zone draw; its wingers stack on the middle
hash marks with a D below them.

- `match.gd setup_faceoff` (end-zone dots only, |z| past the blue line + 6 m):
  - defending wingers stand one behind the other on the goal side of the
    middle hash marks (`FACEOFF_HASH_X` 4.6, `FACEOFF_HASH_Z` 0.6,
    `FACEOFF_STACK` 1.1 m);
  - one defending D stands below them toward the net;
  - the other defending D stands low behind the centre on the boards side;
  - attacking wingers line up on the blue-line side of the hashes;
  - attacking D stay at the blue line.
- Everyone is kept in front of the goal line. Neutral-zone and centre-ice
  lineups are unchanged.
- New `tests/test_faceoff_alignment.gd` covers both end dots.
