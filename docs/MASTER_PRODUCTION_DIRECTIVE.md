## Full production coverage

Audit and acceptance must cover skating edges/acceleration/glide/crossovers/turns/backwards/pivots/stops/contact recovery/fatigue; momentum, IK, foot contact and transitions; 3D puck/stick physics, receiving, deflections, rebounds, battles and protection; mass/velocity/strength/balance/bracing/angle/boards/legality contact; goalie depth/tracking/butterfly/recovery/posts/screens/deflections/rebounds/covers/handling/desperation/styles; faceoffs/lines/fatigue/rules/manpower/OT/shootouts/empty-net/delayed penalties/timeouts/statistics.

Coaching must cover forechecks, neutral formations, breakouts, offense/defense coverage, pressure/aggression, passing/shots, pair behavior/matchups, special teams, faceoffs and late-game risk with debug intentions. Difficulty must use fair decisions and execution, not hidden physics changes. Ratings/tendencies and bounded original signature abilities must produce measurable differences and counterplay.

Equipment coverage includes skater sticks/blade patterns/handedness/helmets/visors/cages/gloves/skates/pants/shells/socks/jerseys/tape and goalkeeper masks/artwork/chest silhouette/catchers/blockers/pads/pants/skates/sticks/straps/colors. Use original designs and fictional brands until appropriate authorization review. Research photographs do not grant product-design rights. Equipment must fit, avoid clipping, animate consistently and persist through editors and saves; cosmetics have no secret advantages.

Career must separate player skill, character development and roster opportunity; include original creation/position/handedness/physical profile/playstyle/equipment/evaluation/scouting/eligibility/contracts/role/line/ice time/training/milestones/stats/awards/records/retirement. Age-appropriate minor competition uses fictional/generated identities. Good positioning, practice and team play drive transparent progression; no stat-padding incentives or arbitrary dialogue objectives.

GM must model multi-season eligibility, restrictions/finances, rosters/depth/lines/contracts/free agency/trades/picks/order/scouting/prospects/coaches/staff/injuries/fatigue/roles/schedules/standings/playoffs/awards/history/records/feeder assignments/retirement/generated prospects. Progression uses age/potential uncertainty/usage/competition/coaching/training/health/environment rather than linear growth. AI transaction tests include positional need, age/value/replacements/window/budget and repeatable exploit prevention. Long simulations check roster legality, scoring, contract/prospect supply and finances.

Practice covers free skate/shoot/pass/breakaway/2-on-1/defense/faceoff/goalie/contact/special-team/custom drills with instructions, instant reset and optional feedback. Play Now covers full setup, pregame, intermission, results/stats/rematch and presets.

Audio covers skate cuts/stops/stick/pass/shot/pad/glove/boards/glass/posts/crossbar/contact/whistles/celebrations/crowd/chants/ambience/announcements, original or licensed only, with spatial variation, priorities and dynamic contextual mixing. Arena reaction depends on home/away/chances/saves/hits/penalties/rivalry/playoffs/late-game/OT.

Presentation covers modern menu/player cards/career and GM dashboards/tables/trades/scouting/draft/scoreboard/penalties/intermissions/replays/highlights/results/records/settings, readable contrast and puck visibility with restrained broadcast interruptions. Do not hide weak models with post-processing. Specialist art, animation, audio, human testing and licensing gaps remain explicit.

Accessibility/input include rebinds/sensitivity/deadzones/hold-toggle/aim-pass-switch assists/device prompts/hotplug/disconnect/vibration/camera/puck cues/text scaling/color-independent cues/reduced motion/audio balance/subtitles. No untested Windows/controller/Steam Deck compatibility claim. Multiplayer, authority/latency/cheating/matchmaking/disconnect/hosting/support are a separately scoped stream; audit first and do not casually promise it.

Acceptance includes actual functionality/integration/save-load/input/error/performance/accessibility/presentation/regression checks and breakaway/2-on-1/screen/deflection/rebound/board/post/delayed penalty/offside/icing/special-team/OT/empty-net/controller/interrupted-save/multi-season scenarios. Human playtests judge feel/fun; passing tests alone does not establish realism. Use current official Steam requirements before release work; no unapproved purchases, agreements, payments or availability changes.

Maintain evidence-based quality gaps in gameplay/animation/art/audio/AI/modes/content/UI/accessibility/stability/performance/pipeline/support/release. Work in meaningful vertical slices. Preserve verified commits, tests/failures, artifacts if genuine, next checkpoint, ownership and integration state. Report actual player-visible improvements or explicitly their absence.
